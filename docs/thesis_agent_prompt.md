# Prompt for the THESIS repository agent

Written for: the agent that edits `~/Code/THESIS` (LaTeX) and needs to know where the DUNE solar-neutrino figures, tables and numbers come from.

---

You are editing the LaTeX thesis in `~/Code/THESIS`. Its figures and tables are generated by a sibling repository, `~/Code/LOWE_RECONSTRUCTION_PUBLICATION` (called LOWE below), from SOLAR analysis outputs. **Never edit generated figures or tables by hand, and never regenerate them from the THESIS side.** Ask for a LOWE rerun instead. The THESIS working tree has uncommitted changes of the author's; do not clobber them (no `git checkout`/`reset`/`stash`).

## Pipeline

1. SOLAR (remote) writes plot-ready pickles under `output/data/analysis/{day-night|hep|sensitivity}/{config}/marley/{folder}/{label}/`.
   `folder` is `truncated` (the thesis reference), `nominal` or `reduced`; `label` is `default` or a study name.
2. `LOWE/scripts/sync_solar_data.sh --yes` copies them into `LOWE/input/data/studies/{folder}/{label}/` (the reference copy of `truncated/default` is also in `LOWE/input/data/`). `--truth-position` fetches the truth-position export into `LOWE/input/data/truth_position/`.
3. `python run_plot_scripts.py -s <list>` and `python run_table_scripts.py -s <list>` in LOWE run `LOWE/input/plots/<list>_scripts.txt` and `LOWE/input/tables/<list>_scripts.txt`. Each line is one call of a macro in `LOWE/scripts/script_*.py`. Output locations come from `LOWE/config/output_paths.json`: `study` and `solar` write to `THESIS/figures/study/<sub-folder>/` and `THESIS/figures/solar/`.
4. `LOWE/input/status/study_status_20260921_v15.md` is SOLAR's status table, and the authority for every quoted number. `python scripts/ch9_check_status.py` in LOWE checks the pickles against it (latest run: 137 rows, 593 checks, 0 mismatches).

## How to quote numbers

- Day-Night and HEP: significance in σ at 20 yr, taken from the `at_eval` column of the status table. Sensitivity: Δχ² (solar-fit-at-reactor / reactor-fit-at-solar) at 10 yr. Figures run to 30 yr (Sensitivity has only 10 yr and 30 yr points).
- Each row uses its own best cut; the cut and fiducial volume are in the table. Where the `status` column says `cut!=ref(...)`, the study was cut differently from the default and the figure must say so.
- **Preliminary rows** (tags `epoch`, `behind_default`, `values_bug`) are not final: those are the Nominal and Reduced `default` rows, `bkg_gamma_*`, `charge_Q50/Q100/Q500` for Day-Night and HEP, and all `unc_*`, `nuisance_*` and `oscpoint_*` rows still marked. Say "preliminary" in the text or caption and do not build a conclusion on them. The Sensitivity `charge_*` rows do not follow the trend of the Day-Night and HEP rows and must not be smoothed or reordered.
- The energy-estimator rows now exist for all three analyses (HEP and Sensitivity, 2026-09-20). Truth-energy HEP is 2.5× (HD central) to 8–10× (VD) the default because the fiducial cut is 0/0/0 and the HEP energy window is ignored. Treat these as upper-bound "perfect energy" numbers, not physics results. The status table gives them no trend verdict. HD lateral `energy_maink` Sensitivity is missing.
- One fresh unexpected row: VD shielded `membrane_veto_off` Sensitivity is 0.34 against 0.40 for the default (expected ≥). It is under evaluation, so do not describe the veto's Sensitivity effect as settled. The Day-Night and HEP veto effects are small (at most +0.13σ, VD shielded HEP).
- The truth-position numbers are raw-weight figures of merit, not oscillation-weighted significances. HD central's FoM-best truth X (320 cm, 16 % efficiency) is not quotable, and the Δχ² unit for Sensitivity is unverified. The caveats are in `LOWE/docs/truth_position_outputs.md`.

## When something looks wrong

Compare the figure with the status table row (config / analysis / study). If they disagree, the pickle has changed and LOWE needs a resync and rerun; report it, do not patch the LaTeX numbers. The file `LOWE/docs/study_data_provenance.md` describes where each family of pickles comes from.
