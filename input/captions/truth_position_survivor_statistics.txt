Surviving MC statistics at the analysis cut per sample: MC events after the cut, after the reco fiducial volume and after the truth pipeline, weight sums, effective statistics and shares of the background weight. Weights are raw SignalParticleWeight only: no oscillation, no MC-support gate, no smoothing. These tables diagnose position information; they are not replacement significances. Window 10--20 MeV, Truncated folder, default best cut of each analysis. The truth pipeline is the truth position with the truth containment cut and, for backgrounds, the position-consistency cut ($|\Delta X|\le100$ cm, $|\Delta Y|,|\Delta Z|\le50$ cm), exactly as the fiduc\_truth study applies it. Radiological has 1--65 MC events depending on the selection, with weights of about $10^5$ each: its fractions and weights are not statistical statements.
